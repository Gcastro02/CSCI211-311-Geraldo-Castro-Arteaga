About Us

The "Why?"

We started FB Mobile Mechanics because we saw how frustrating the traditional repair shop model is. Towing your car, waiting in a lobby for hours, and dealing with hidden fees just didn't make sense. We believe auto repair should be transparent, convenient, and built around your schedule.


Our mission is to bring dealership-quality service directly to your driveway, ensuring you know exactly what is going on with your vehicle every step of the way.
